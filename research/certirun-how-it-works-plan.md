# CertiRun How It Works Plan

## Page Job

How It Works should make the sourcing process visible. Each step should show what the buyer gives, what CertiRun does, what output is produced, and what decision the buyer can make.

## Recommended Step Model

| Step | Buyer Input | CertiRun Action | Visible Output | Buyer Decision Point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Requirement Intake | product need, photos, drawings, BOM, quantity, market | clarify missing details | RFQ brief | approve scope for supplier outreach |
| 2. Supplier Search | approved RFQ brief | contact and screen suppliers | supplier response list | choose suppliers to compare |
| 3. Quotation Comparison | supplier replies | normalize price, MOQ, lead time, terms, documents | comparison sheet | shortlist option or request more info |
| 4. Verification Checkpoint | samples, photos, documents, certificates | review visible details and flag uncertainty | checkpoint notes | proceed, revise, or reject |
| 5. Order Coordination | selected supplier and order details | track labels, packing, payment, production notes | order detail record | confirm production/shipment readiness |
| 6. Delivery Follow-Up | destination and shipping requirements | follow domestic logistics, export handover, documents | dispatch/document log | accept handover or request issue follow-up |

## Copy Direction

Use buyer-facing language. Avoid process jargon unless it helps the buyer understand a concrete output.

## Proof Direction

Where possible, show example artifacts without exposing private information:

- RFQ brief format
- supplier comparison fields
- packing checklist
- document checklist
- dispatch update log
