# CertiRun About Plan

## About Page Job

The About page should explain why CertiRun is good at sourcing execution, not just who the founder is.

## Positioning Direction

Turn founder background into operating advantages:

1. Requirement Clarification
2. Document & Detail Control
3. Traceable Coordination

## Recommended Message

CertiRun's value comes from structured verification, document review, multi-party communication, and follow-through. The page should show how that operating method reduces buyer uncertainty.

## Avoid

- claiming expertise across all product categories
- implying official authorization
- using vague claims like "we know every factory"
- over-relying on personal biography
- implying certification, inspection, or technical authority beyond evidence

## Suggested Section Order

1. Why CertiRun exists
2. The sourcing problems it is built to reduce
3. The operating method: clarify, compare, verify, follow through
4. Three buyer-facing advantages
5. Boundaries and honest sourcing language
6. Contact/RFQ CTA
