# CertiRun Positioning

## Role

CertiRun is a service-driven China sourcing execution site. It helps overseas buyers turn incomplete sourcing requirements into a clearer RFQ, comparable supplier responses, visible checkpoints, and an order-follow-up path.

CertiRun should feel like an operating partner for uncertain sourcing work, not a catalog, marketplace, or ecommerce store.

## Buyer Served

CertiRun serves buyers who:

- know the type of product they need but do not have a clean sourcing path
- need supplier search, comparison, or verification support
- need help preparing RFQs from photos, drawings, BOMs, samples, or partial specifications
- need communication, packing, document, and delivery follow-up
- buy across electronics, machinery, hardware, selected vehicle-parts sourcing scenarios, and new energy / PV / EV categories

## Problems Solved

- Requirement clarification before supplier outreach
- Supplier comparison across price, MOQ, lead time, document response, and communication quality
- Verification checkpoints for photos, samples, documents, labels, packing, and shipment readiness
- Coordination across suppliers, domestic logistics, consolidation, and export steps
- Buyer-side visibility through traceable outputs rather than vague promises

## Not In Scope

CertiRun should not:

- become a broad SKU catalog
- publish long-tail part-number pages
- imply verified offers without evidence
- publish price, stock, lead time, MOQ, certification, authorization, or fitment claims without review
- copy supplier marketing text or third-party product descriptions
- use fake cases, fake customer names, fake certificates, or fake shipment records

## Blog Status

Blog / Insights is a live CertiRun module. It should support sourcing-process intent, RFQ education, supplier comparison, QC, document control, logistics, Incoterms, MOQ, payment terms, and category-specific buying notes.

## CertiSpares Status For Phase One

Cross-site routing to CertiSpares is deferred. Do not add new CertiSpares links, CTAs, or explanatory brand-relationship copy in this phase.
