# CertiRun Cross-Linking Policy

## Phase One Decision

Cross-site routing to CertiSpares is deferred. Even though the business-control documents allow intent-aware links between CertiRun and CertiSpares, this phase should not add new CertiSpares links, CTAs, or brand-relationship explanations.

## Allowed In Phase One

- Internal links within CertiRun
- Home to Services, Industries, How It Works, Cases, Blog, About, Contact
- Industry pages to Contact/RFQ
- Blog articles to relevant CertiRun service and industry pages
- Cases to Contact/RFQ and relevant CertiRun categories

## Not Allowed In Phase One

- New CertiSpares CTAs
- New CertiSpares navigation links
- New explanatory copy about the relationship between CertiRun and CertiSpares
- New vehicle-parts deep-intent routing copy
- Duplicate content designed to compete with CertiSpares intent

## Future Reopen Conditions

When cross-site routing is reopened, evaluate:

1. Does the buyer intent clearly shift from sourcing-process to part/RFQ intent?
2. Does the page mention OE, part number, cross reference, replacement, aftermarket, or fitment?
3. Would adding a link reduce confusion rather than blur brand roles?
4. Is the link contextual and limited rather than sitewide?

Until then, keep CertiRun self-contained.
