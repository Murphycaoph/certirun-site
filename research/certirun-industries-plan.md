# CertiRun Industries Plan

## Standard Template

Each industry page should keep a consistent service-landing structure:

1. Hero and positioning
2. What we help source
3. Typical buyer requests
4. How we support
5. What to send with your RFQ
6. Common sourcing risks
7. Contact/RFQ CTA

## Category-Specific RFQ Fields

### Electronics & Electronic Components

- part number or model
- datasheet
- voltage / current
- connector type
- label and packing requirement
- certificate requirement when needed

### Machinery & Industrial Equipment

- machine type
- working purpose
- capacity
- voltage / power / phase
- spare parts requirement
- packing and installation notes

### Hardware Products

- drawing or product photo
- size
- material
- surface treatment
- quantity per item
- packaging or label requirement

### Commercial & Passenger Vehicle Parts

Keep this page high-level in phase one. It may discuss sourcing support, supplier comparison, packing checks, and buyer-confirm-required fitment risk, but should not expand into OE, part number, fitment, cross-reference, or SKU-depth pages.

### New Energy / PV / EV Parts

- product name
- technical specification
- voltage / current / power rating
- datasheet
- certificate or standard requirement
- project or installation context

## Shared Risk Language

Use qualified language where facts need confirmation:

- subject to supplier confirmation
- buyer confirmation required
- certificate information must be checked before order
- compatibility and specification require review before purchase
