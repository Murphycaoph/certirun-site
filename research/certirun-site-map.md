# CertiRun Site Map

Current navigation should remain simple and buyer-action oriented:

```text
Home
Services
Industries
How It Works
Cases
Insights
About
Contact
```

`Insights` is the existing Blog module and should remain active.

## Internal Link Direction

- Home links to Services, Industries, How It Works, Cases, Blog, and Contact.
- Industry pages link to Contact/RFQ and relevant Services sections.
- Blog articles link to relevant service or industry pages where they support buyer education.
- Cases link to Contact/RFQ and, where useful, the relevant category page.
- No phase-one CertiSpares routing.

## URL Strategy

Use short, stable, readable English URLs. Do not create artificial deep URL silos just for SEO. Internal links and clear page intent matter more than excessive folder nesting.
